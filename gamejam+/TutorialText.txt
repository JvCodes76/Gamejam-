Hello! Welcome! You, my friend, are a witch's apprentice, and monsters are invading your forest! Use WASD to move around.

Shocker, but those monsters really want to hurt you! Don't worry though, you have your potions to defend yourself! Press M1 to shoot where you're looking.

Wow! Two items dropped!! Go up to them and press E to pick them up! If your bag is full, you can open it at any time with TAB and every item nearby will show up there, on the ground.

Every time you pick up an item (or get a synergy but we'll get tp that), it goes into the journal on the bottom right hand side of the screen!

There, you can see what they do and a little description (that I frankly put a lot of love into :3), so check it out!

This is your trusty cauldron, every graveyard has one, obviously. Press E to interact with it.

This is the cauldron Screen! To make a new potion, drag the ingredients you want to use into the cauldron and hold ENTER

When the potion is done brewing, press F to bottle it up.

You're only an apprendice, so you just know the basics of every ingredient, to find out how they synergise and interact, experiment!

That's the basics! Now go out there and get'em! And if you fail, remember, there's always the... next hex!!! Please laugh.
